--- Keybinds
The following keybinds have been automatically applied to the Space Station 14 installation.

-- Controlling speed
Alt+1 - 1x Speed (30 TPS)
Alt+2 - 2x Speed (60 TPS)
Alt+3 - 3x Speed (90 TPS)
Alt+4 - 4x Speed (120 TPS)
Alt+Q - 8x Speed (240 TPS)

-- Seeking
Alt+A - Skip 5 seconds backwards
Alt+Shift+A - Skip 1 minute backwards
Alt+D - Skip 5 seconds forwards
Alt+Shift+D - Skip 1 minute forwards

-- Utility
Alt+F - Toggle Fullbright
